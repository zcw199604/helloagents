# 数据模型

## Skill 元数据

每个 `SKILL.md` frontmatter 至少包含:

- `name`
- `description`
- `invocation`
- `side_effects`
- `requires`
- `completion_criteria`

## 目录模型

- `Codex/Skills/CN/skills/helloagents/<skill>/SKILL.md`
- `Codex/Skills/CN/skills/helloagents/<skill>/references/*.md`
- `Claude/Skills/CN/skills/helloagents/<skill>/SKILL.md`
- `Claude/Skills/CN/skills/helloagents/<skill>/references/*.md`
